Research briefing & model assumptions

This fictional seedling model explores a possible biological response to warmer growing conditions. It does not establish whether climate change is occurring or predict actual crop yields.

Daily height growth follows a curved temperature response, highest near 24°C, multiplied by water and light response factors. Growth slows at high temperatures. Individual growth potential and daily measurement variation create overlapping results. No mortality, nutrient limitation, acclimation, or temperature–water interactions are modeled.

Growth formula: 0.65 × exp(−((temperature − 24)/10)²) × min(water/50, 1.15) × min(light/12, 1.1) × individual potential, plus small daily variation; negative increments are limited to zero. This is an instructional model, not a calibrated empirical dataset.

A reference group provides a baseline, even though its plants receive water and light. Repeated observations of one plant are not additional independent plants. Random assignment helps distribute pre-existing differences; it does not guarantee identical groups.
